How the signal works

The purpose of this indicator is not to predict the exact bottom. It asks a simpler question: has the declining USD/CNY trend actually ended?

The trend timescale is learned from the historical data. There is no fixed 20-day window.

Trend Z is the estimated trend divided by its statistical uncertainty. A negative value means USD/CNY is falling. A positive value means it is rising.

Reversal evidence measures how strongly the trend has moved away from its previous negative state. It accumulates when the trend improves and disappears when the decline resumes.

WAIT means the decline is still statistically present. BOTTOMING means the decline has weakened substantially but has not yet produced a confirmed reversal. BUY means the trend has crossed from negative to positive with sufficient historical evidence.

The same UTC cutoff is used for every visitor. Therefore someone viewing this page in California and someone viewing it in China will receive the same dataset and the same signal.
