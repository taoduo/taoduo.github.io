The rule

Buy USD when the accumulated evidence that the USD/CNY downtrend has ended becomes stronger than the level that was historically associated with a good purchase point.

The algorithm does not assume a fixed 20-day future, a fixed 20-day trend, or a manually chosen Z-score threshold.
What is optimized?

Every historical date is treated as though it were "today." The algorithm only sees observations preceding that date.

A hypothetical USD purchase is then compared with the eventual bottom of that declining episode. Buying before the decline has finished is penalized heavily; waiting until after the bottom is penalized according to how much USD/CNY subsequently recovered.

The parameters producing the lowest historical loss are selected and then applied to today's data.
Data source: Frankfurter daily USD/CNY observations, beginning November 3, 2025.

This is a statistical timing rule, not a prediction that identifies the exact market bottom. Exchange rates can change regime abruptly.
